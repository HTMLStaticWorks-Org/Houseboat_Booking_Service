Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you set sail.

What is included in the houseboat package?
All packages include accommodation, all meals prepared on board, a dedicated crew, and guided navigation along your chosen route. Specific inclusions vary by package — see the Boats & Packages page for full details.
Can I customise the route and itinerary?
Absolutely. Our routes are starting points, not fixed tracks. Tell us your interests — wildlife, local markets, photography spots, quiet anchorages — and we'll build a day around them.
What is the best time of year to visit?
October through March offers the most comfortable conditions — warm days, calm waters and clear skies. The monsoon season (June–August) has its own dramatic beauty if you enjoy the rains.
How do I book and what is the cancellation policy?
Use the Contact page or email us directly to place a booking enquiry. We'll confirm availability and walk you through payment and cancellation terms before any commitment is made.
