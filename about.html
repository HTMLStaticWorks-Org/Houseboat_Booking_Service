Our Heritage

Tradition shaped by the currents of time

Before houseboats became sanctuaries for travellers, they were kettuvallams — large wooden craft that carried grain and spices across Kerala's vast canal networks. Each was hand-tied using coir rope, treated with fish oils, and built completely without nails.

At Lagoon & Leaf, we preserve this master craft. Our vessels are built and restored by multi-generational Alappuzha boat artisans, updated only with the comforts you need to rest deeply.

  • Hand-stitched Anjili wood hulls
  • Locally woven bamboo and coir interiors
  • Quiet, eco-conscious propulsion
  • 100% indigenous local staff
What Guides Us

Our Core Commitments

Every decision we make, from the routes we choose to the spices we grind, is rooted in these four principles.

🐢

The Rhythm of Slow Travel

We don't rush between checklist destinations. We moor beneath swaying coconut palms, drift past sleepy tea stalls, and let the backwaters set the pace.

🌱

Ecological Stewardship

The backwaters are our home and our livelihood. We practice strict zero-plastic waste on board, utilize solar power where possible, and treat waterways with reverence.

🤝

Community Vitality

100% of our captains, chefs, and service teams come from Alappuzha and surrounding villages. We buy fish directly from canoe fishermen and vegetables from canal farmers.

🍲

Authentic Coastal Hospitality

Kerala's hospitality is world-renowned for a reason. You aren't simply a passenger; you are an honoured guest in our waterborne sanctuary.
